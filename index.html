How NutriCheck Works

1. Target recognition

A target-specific aptamer or enzyme recognises the selected adulterant.

2. Signal generation

The interaction produces a measurable colourimetric signal on the biosensor strip.

3. Smartphone analysis

The camera captures the strip and the app analyses its colour/intensity against calibration values.

4. Preliminary result

The app displays a screening category and stores the test record.
